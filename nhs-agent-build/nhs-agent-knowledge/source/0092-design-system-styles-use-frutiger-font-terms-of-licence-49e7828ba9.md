# Terms of the licence

This is a licence for specific Frutiger fonts for NHS organisations only. By registering for this licence, you can get access to authorised versions of the Frutiger font.

Before you can download and use the Frutiger font under NHS England's licence, you must read and agree to these terms (including the Annex: detailed terms, which you can find a link to within the registration form). As set out at the start of the registration form, by clicking "agree" you confirm that you are authorised to accept these terms (including the detailed terms in the Annex) on behalf of your organisation. Where we refer to "you" or "your" in the rest of these terms, we are therefore referring to your organisation.

NHS England has a licence for 3 weights of the Frutiger font, which is the brand font for the NHS. Monotype owns the font software and other elements of the font which are protected by intellectual property rights. We call these the "font" in these terms.

## Who can register for a licence

NHS organisations in England can register to use the font for free under this licence.

You do not need to register if your team is part of NHS England.

The legal effects of the terms of the licence depend on what type of organisation you are.

This licence is one of the following:

- an "NHS contract" (as defined in section 9 of the National Health Service Act 2006 ) when made with NHS trusts (and other NHS organisations listed in that section, such as integrated care boards) – however, this licence should still be treated with the same degree of rigour and seriousness as if it were legally binding

- a legally binding agreement when made with organisations where the licence does not fall within the definition of an NHS contract (such as foundation trusts)

You agree that NHS England has the right at any time to assign, replace with a new contract with our successor (or novate the contract), subcontract or otherwise transfer, without notice, all or any part of this agreement and all or any of its rights and obligations under it to any successor (including a successor body of all or part of its functions).

## The fonts covered by this licence

The licence covers Frutiger in 3 weights:

- Frutiger 65 Bold

- Frutiger 55 Roman

- Frutiger 45 Light, but the licence does not cover using this weight in an app

This licence covers the font version and format that NHS England bought in December 2024. It does not cover any newer versions of the font.

You are allowed to use the font as described in this licence, alongside other organisations to which we licence the font.

### Other Frutiger fonts

You must buy your own licence if you want to use:

- any other font or font weight, such as Frutiger Italic

- Frutiger 45 Light in a mobile downloadable app

- Frutiger in a way that is not covered in this licence

If you have a licence for other Frutiger fonts, you must follow the terms of that licence.

### Permitted formats

Use of the font is only permitted in formats which are known and in existence at the time of NHS England's licence from Monotype (for example, in emails, websites, printed materials, apps, word processing programs, PDFs, and so on). This means that this licence does not cover new formats (such as variable fonts, which are fonts that use 1 file to store many font styles), nor does it allow you to use the font in any new or innovative formats (for example, in any entirely new technologies invented after the end of 2024).
